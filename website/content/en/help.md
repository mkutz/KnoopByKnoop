# Help

Knoop by Knoop plans and navigates routes on the knooppunt networks: the numbered junctions ("knots") for cycling and hiking. Along these networks every knot has a number, and signs point to the neighbouring numbers. You plan a route as a list of numbers, and the app tells you the next one while you ride or walk.


## Planning a route

- Choose **Cycling** or **Hiking** at the top of the map.
- Tap a numbered knot for the start, then another for the end. The route follows the network between them, along the actual paths.
- Tap more knots to add stops; each goes where it fits along the route. Move or remove stops in the list of knots, and use **Undo** to take back any change.
- **Search a place** to find a village or a sight, or show the **Nearest knots** around you.
- The panel shows the route's length and the knots in riding order, each with the village it lies in.


## Navigating

- Tap the green **Navigate** button.
- If you are not at the start, the app asks whether to go to the start or to start at a knot near you.
- At every knot the app tells you the number to follow next, by voice and on the lock screen, so the screen can stay off.
- When you leave the route or go the wrong way, it warns you and guides you back.
- Tap **Stop** to end the navigation.


## Sharing a route

- Tap **Share** in the panel. **Route for Knoop by Knoop** sends a small file that friends with the app can open; **GPX** is for other apps and devices.
- Open such a file from Files, mail, a messenger or AirDrop, and the app shows the route, ready to go.


## No knots on the map

The knot networks the app knows are in the **Netherlands**, **Belgium** and **North Rhine-Westphalia** (Germany). Elsewhere the map shows no knots.

- Move the map to one of these areas, or **Search a place** there, for example "Utrecht", "Ghent" or "Kinderdijk".
- Zoom in: the knots show from a certain zoom level.
- On the first start the app downloads the networks; this needs a connection for a few seconds.


## Without a connection

The networks, the map along your route and the paths around it are stored on your phone, so planning and riding work without a connection. Searching places and the map away from your route need one.


## Contact

Found a mistake, or missing something? Write to [mail@knoopbyknoop.com](mailto:mail@knoopbyknoop.com) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/mkutz/knoopbyknoop/issues).
Wrong or missing knots and paths are best fixed in [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org), where the app's data comes from; the app picks up changes within about a week.
