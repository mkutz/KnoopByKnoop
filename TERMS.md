## Map and network data

- **From OpenStreetMap.** The knot networks, the ways and the ferry details come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). They are updated about once a week and can be incomplete, outdated or wrong: knots move, paths close, ferries change their times.
- **Ferry times and closures.** Opening times, fees and the warnings about ferries are taken from the data as tagged. Check them with the operator before you rely on a ferry.
- **Guidance away from the route.** The ways back to the route are suggestions from the data. They may lead over paths that are closed, private or unsuitable at that moment.
- **Services of others.** The base map, place search and data downloads come from services listed in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). They may be unavailable at times; the app then works with what is already on the device.
